How to read the visualization

Each row is a list, with its head on the left. Matching numbers and colors identify the same value, even when it appears in multiple rotation lists. These are temporary copies, not extra queue elements.

Current: a stores recent inserts newest-first; b stores the oldest remaining elements. When full with b=[], one insert reverses all of a, evicts its oldest element, and starts a new a.

Hood-Melville: inserts push onto rear; evictions pop front. When the rear outgrows the logical front, a rotation captures both lists. The live front continues serving evictions while a fresh rear accepts inserts. front_len includes elements still being assembled.

Rotation states: reversing copies a front/rear pair per step, then appending prepends the surviving old front. valid says how many copied old-front elements still belong. Crossed-out copies are excluded; eviction lowers valid or removes the head of an already-assembled replacement.

Use each column's Replay / Next controls to inspect internal checkpoints of the most recent insert. The top row always shows the completed result; checkpoints can temporarily have one fewer item during eviction. Replay does not modify the committed buffer. Both implementations are immutable: updates return new versions.
